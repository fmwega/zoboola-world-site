Zoboola World Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 23, 2026

Zoboola World creates educational animated videos and uses a web-based workflow to prepare and manage approved social media content. This policy describes information that may be processed through the TikTok integration.

Information processed

When an authorized account owner connects TikTok, the integration may process TikTok account identifiers, basic profile information made available by approved permissions, authorization tokens, and video upload metadata. The workflow also processes approved video files and operational logs needed for publishing and troubleshooting.

Purposes

Information is used to authenticate the authorized TikTok account, prepare and upload approved videos, detect duplicate submissions, and troubleshoot technical errors. The integration is not intended to sell TikTok account data or use it for advertising profiling.

Service providers

The workflow may use n8n and Google Drive for automation and storage, and TikTok for account authorization and content uploads. Video production may involve additional service providers. Information is shared only as necessary for these operations and subject to the services' applicable terms.

Storage, security, and retention

Access to workflow credentials and operational data is restricted to authorized operators. Reasonable security measures are used to protect information. Operational records and authorization data are kept only while needed for authorized workflows, security, and troubleshooting, subject to applicable legal obligations.

Your choices and deletion

The account owner can revoke TikTok authorization through TikTok account settings. To request information about stored data or its deletion, contact zoboolaworld@gmail.com. Some records may be retained where required by law or for legitimate security purposes.

Children's privacy

Zoboola World makes family-oriented educational content. The TikTok integration is intended for use by an authorized adult account owner and is not designed to collect personal information directly from children.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the integration or its data practices change. The effective date will be updated accordingly.
